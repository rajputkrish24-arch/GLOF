About the GLOF Early Warning & Safe Evacuation System

Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs) present a grave natural hazard across high-altitude mountain basins in the Himalayas. Sudden breaches of moraine dams can discharge millions of cubic meters of water, destroying downstream infrastructure, settlements, and lives.

Core System Features & Architecture

1. Glacial Basin Telemetry

Continuously monitors water levels, surface expansion area, precipitation, and ambient melt temperatures for critical Himalayan lakes (e.g. Chorabari Lake, South Lhonak Lake, Imja Tsho).

2. Evacuation Safety Algorithm

Calculates multi-path route risk scores (0–100) factoring in flood zones, road blockages, active disaster reports, and critical perimeters to recommend the safest evacuation corridor.

3. User Incident Reporting

Empowers field users and citizens to submit verified disaster reports (cloudbursts, landslides, road damage) with GPS coordinates and optional photo evidence.

4. Machine Learning Classification

RandomForest Classifier predictive engine trained on multi-parameter environmental features to evaluate GLOF outburst probability.
